# Israeli Higher Education Institutions - Complete Reference

> Last updated: February 2026
> Sources: CHE (Council for Higher Education), Wikipedia, institution websites, CHE official registry

Israel's higher education system comprises approximately **63 accredited institutions** under the Council for Higher Education (CHE/MALAG), plus additional non-accredited preparatory programs.

### Notes on Universities
- The **8 research universities** are: Hebrew University, Technion, Tel Aviv, Bar-Ilan, Ben-Gurion, Haifa, Weizmann, and Ariel.
- **Weizmann Institute** is unique: graduate-only (MSc/PhD), exclusively natural/exact sciences, full scholarships for all students.
- **Open University** uses open-admissions distance learning model; ~47,000 students across 7 campuses + 50 study centers.
- **Reichman University** (formerly IDC Herzliya) became Israel's first private university in 2021 -- see Private Colleges section as it's sometimes categorized differently by CHE.

### Notes on Budgeted Colleges
- These colleges are funded by the Planning and Budgeting Committee (PBC/VATAT).
- **Bezalel**, **JAMD**, and **Shenkar** are Israel's premier art/design/music institutions.
- **SCE**, **Braude**, **Afeka**, **Azrieli**, and **HIT** focus on engineering.
- **Ruppin** is the only institution offering Marine Sciences BSc in Israel.
- **Neri Bloomfield Academy of Design** (Haifa) merged with University of Haifa in 2024 and is no longer a standalone institution.

- **Reichman University** achieved university status in 2021 -- the first (and so far only) private university in Israel. CHE still lists it under non-budgeted colleges in some references.

- **College of Management (COLMAN)** is the largest by number of programs (~50+).
- Tuition at private colleges is significantly higher than budgeted institutions.

### Notes on Colleges of Education
- These award **B.Ed** and **M.Ed** degrees. Some also offer B.A. programs.
- **Kibbutzim College** (Seminar HaKibbutzim) is the largest, with ~6,000 students.
- **Levinsky-Wingate** is a 2022 merger of Levinsky College of Education and Wingate Academic College.
- **Neri Bloomfield** merged with University of Haifa starting 2024 academic year.
- Several serve specific sectors: Al-Qasemi and Arab Academic College (Arab sector), Shaanan/Talpiot/Herzog/Hemdat Hadarom (religious sector).

## 5. Other Institutions

### 5a. Pre-Academic Preparatory Programs (Mechinot Kadam-Akademiot)

Most universities and many colleges operate their own pre-academic preparatory programs (mechinot). These are NOT degree-granting institutions but serve as bridge programs:

| Institution Offering Mechina | Notes |
|------------------------------|-------|
| Hebrew University Mechina | Year-long, humanities/sciences tracks |
| Bar-Ilan University Mechina | General education + Judaic studies |
| Ben-Gurion University Mechina | Engineering/sciences prep |
| Tel Aviv University Mechina | Various tracks |
| University of Haifa Mechina | Various tracks |
| Ariel University Mechina | Various tracks |
| Most academic colleges | Many colleges run pre-academic tracks |

### 5b. Pre-Military Academies (Mechinot Kdam-Tzva'iot)

There are **54 accredited pre-military mechinot** under the Joint Council of Pre-Military Academies (JCM). These are not academic institutions but are culturally significant:

- Mechina Olamit (World Bnei Akiva)
- Mechinat Haemek
- Mechinat Rabin
- Mechinat Nachshon
- Ein Prat Mechina
- Many others
